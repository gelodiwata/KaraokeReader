Install on iPad or iPhone

Add Read Along to your Home Screen for instant offline reading:

1
Tap the Share button (square with an arrow pointing up ⎋) in the Safari toolbar.
2
Scroll down and select Add to Home Screen (⊞).
3
Tap Add in the top right corner. The app will launch fullscreen as an independent app from your home screen!
